About Me

I am a Computer Scientist and AI researcher, currently serving as Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Informatics at the University of Energy and Natural Resources, Ghana.

My research focuses on deep learning, computer vision and lightweight neural network architectures — applied to real-world problems in health, agriculture, climate, and environmental sustainability across Africa. I have co-authored research publications spanning AI, dataset development and medical imaging, and serve as a reviewer for PLOS ONE, Scientific Reports, BMC Medical Imaging and Memetic Computing.

I am a Schmidt AI in Science Faculty Fellow (first cohort 2025–2028) at the University of Oxford and a two-time recipient of the International Development Research Centre (IDRC) grant. I am passionate about mentoring the next generation of computer scientists and empowering young girls through ICT programs across Ghana.
